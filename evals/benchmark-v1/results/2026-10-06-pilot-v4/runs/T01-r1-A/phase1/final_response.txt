```python
if numstr[-2:] in ('11', '12', '13'):
    ext = 'th'
else:
    ext = _ORDINAL_MAP.get(numstr[-1], 'th')
```

The slice-based check subsumes the old single-digit `IndexError` path, so string inputs, non-digit-ending inputs, `ext_only=True`, and larger numbers behave as before. Docstring examples now also cover teens and twenties.
