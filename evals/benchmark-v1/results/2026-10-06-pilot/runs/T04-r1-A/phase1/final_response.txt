## Summary

**Implementation — `boltons/strutils.py:886` (`parse_int_list`)**
- Replaced naive `split(range_delim)` parsing with two regexes: a signed-integer pattern (`[+-]?\d+`) and an inclusive signed-range pattern that allows whitespace around the range delimiter and both endpoints.
- Handles `-3--1` → `[-3, -2, -1]`, `+2-+4` → `[2, 3, 4]`, cross-zero ranges (`1--3`), and reversed endpoints (`-1--3`, `+4-+2`) via `min`/`max`.
- `re.escape(range_delim)` keeps custom delimiters literal (e.g. `..`, `|`, `to`), and `delim`/`range_delim` behave exactly as before for custom values.
- Sorting, duplicate preservation, empty input/tokens, and leading/trailing delimiters are unchanged.
- Malformed tokens (`1-`, `-`, `1-2-3`, `--1`, `1..3` with default delimiters, `a-1`, etc.) now raise `ValueError` instead of mis-parsing.
- Docstring updated: signed/reversed/whitespace semantics, a `Raises:` section, and new doctests, all passing.
