**Root cause** — in `boltons/strutils.py`, `ordinalize` checked `numstr[-2] == '2'` under the comment "first check for teens". The teens rule keys on a tens digit of `1` (11–19 take `th`). The inverted check caused:
- 11, 12, 13 → `11st`, `12nd`, `13rd` (teens fell through to the final-digit map)
- 21, 22, 23 → `21th`, `22th`, `23th` (twenties wrongly forced to `th`)

**Fix** (`boltons/strutils.py:172`) — changed the check to `numstr[-2] == '1'`, restoring the standard rule: last two digits in 11–19 → `th`, otherwise suffix follows the final digit. The `ext_only`, single-digit, and non-digit-trailing behaviors are untouched (`try/except IndexError` path and `ext` initialization unchanged).
